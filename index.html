How does the Round of 32 work?

The 2026 World Cup has 12 groups (A–L). Top 2 from each group plus the 8 best third-placed teams advance — that's 32 teams in the Round of 32, paired in 16 matches.

FIFA labels each R32 slot with a group position code:

  • 1A = winner of Group A
  • 2A = runner-up of Group A
  • 3ABCDF = a best-third-placed team from one of Groups A / B / C / D / F

So a card like 2A vs 2B means the runner-up of Group A plays the runner-up of Group B. Click the card to open the picker — it shows every team that could fill either slot (8 teams for a simple match like 2A vs 2B, up to ~24 for a match with a best-third slot). Pick the team you think wins.

Your pick:

  • shows up highlighted on the card
  • automatically advances into the right R16 slot
  • can be changed any time before the lockout — click it again to clear

R16 → QF → SF → Final all cascade from your R32 picks. The picker only opens for R32; for later rounds, click directly on either of the two teams in the match.

Scoring: +2 per correct R32 pick, +3 per correct R16 / QF / SF / Final pick, +10 bonus if your popup champion is the actual tournament winner.
